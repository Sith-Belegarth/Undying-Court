Wrap-Up

The table leaves the crypt. Malqor and Seraphyne may be destroyed, driven off, or merely disturbed. The immediate hooks resolve (warlord paid off, altar cleansed or fed, guild debt settled), but the larger premise doesn't have to end. The Court's corruption was strong enough to raise two rulers from beyond death; it's reasonable that it isn't finished.

Turning This Into a Campaign

  • If the boss survives: Malqor's phylactery or Seraphyne's true coffin is still out there. A sequel adventure is a hunt for it.
  • If Shaen Orvo's pregen was in the party: her "Persistent Nodes" research reframes the whole dungeon as one node among several across the multiverse. The natural next arc is chasing the pattern to wherever it repeats next.
  • If Lethari's pregen was in the party: the Court's corruption "spreading into the natural world" gives you an overland arc. Blighted beasts and blackened vines lead to wherever you want to take the party next.
  • If the Vess/Tey'orah secret is still buried: let it surface in a quieter, character-focused session before the next dungeon crawl.
  • If the Raven Queen pattern went unnoticed: a later session can reveal that Kaelor, Malrik, and Zyra were each nudged toward this dungeon by the same divine hand. This raises the question of what the goddess of endings wanted with the Undying Court in the first place.
  • Loose threads to seed now, pay off later: the warlord's other lost ancestral blades; the thieves' guild's other rumored vaults; Selvos's unfinished copy of the Odyssey of the Dragonlords; Sarthos Kain's double life; whoever built the Court's crystal-powered sconces and whether that knowledge still exists elsewhere.
