Part Three: The Court in Ruin (Dining Hall / Arena)

Other, smaller drains are impassable. The party may exit the sewers into the dining hall, where the next large drain is located, to explore the ruins of the court. There are hints throughout of the royals' perversions.

Once the monarchs' great blood-sport arena, its stone benches now sag under decades of moss and silt. The long dining table nearby is set with corroded silverware, as if a feast were interrupted mid-course and never resumed. Rusted manacles hang from the arena's support columns. The mosaics clue from the Antechamber pays off directly here. This is what they depicted.

Suggested Content

  • A Barbarian's hook payoff: a single surviving ancestral trophy rack, empty but for one weapon. The ancestral blade the Fighter's warlord wants, or a hint toward it.
  • One easy roleplay/investigation beat: a preserved menu or ledger (Investigation DC 12) naming the "guests" who never left, seeding NPC names.

Undead Arena Champions. The arena's dead answer the call to fight once more. Statues and shackled remains alike wrench free of moss and rust, taking up whatever's left of their old weapons (x2 champions). Fittingly for the room, they don't rise silently — the crowd's roar seems to echo with them.

Statline: reskin the two remaining Broken Guardians from the Antechamber (see Part One — Animated Armor stat block, AC 18, HP 33 (6d8+6), Multiattack: two Slam attacks, 1d6+2 each) as arena champions rather than temple statues. Same stats, new flavor.

This is the party's second taste of the animated guardians — a full pair now, after the single warning shot in the Antechamber. Drawn by noise from the sewers onward.

Rot-Touched Hounds (Optional/Alternate)

Feral dogs that never left the arena, diseased and half-rotted but still hunting in a pack. Swap in for (or add alongside) the Arena Champions if you want a faster, bloodier fight. Use SRD Wolf as-is; the "rot-touched" flavor is cosmetic.

CR1/4 each
AC13
HP11 (2d8+2)
Speed40 ft.
Attack+4 to hit
Damage2d4+2 piercing

Pack Tactics. Advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the hound's allies is within 5 ft. of the target and isn't incapacitated — already suits running 2–3 hounds together. A target knocked prone by a bite (DC 11 Strength save) is easy pickings for the rest of the pack.

From here, the party moves toward the throne room and the ritual hall beyond, leading finally to the crypt.

The Treasury (Side Room, off the Dining Hall)

Three separate hooks — Rogue, Wizard, and Sorcerer — all point at a treasury or vault. This is that room. Place it as a locked or hidden side door off the Dining Hall (fits the Rogue's "paid for vault access" hook directly) or off the corridor leading to the Ritual Hall, whichever suits your table's pacing. It doesn't need to be large — a single vault chamber the party can choose to detour into is enough to pay off all three hooks without padding the dungeon.

Design Note

This room is the direct payoff for the Foreshadowing callout in Part One: the sconce crystals and the sword crystals are confirmed the same substance, and this is where that substance (and the knowledge to work it) actually turns up. If you'd rather not add a room, redirect all three hooks to the crypt loot instead (see Treasure below, Part Four) — just be explicit with the players that the crystals they've been tracking are down there, so the hook still resolves.

Part Four: Battle in the Crypt

This level contains the ruins of the throne room. It overlooks the dining hall and leads to a ritual hall where waking the preserved king and queen is depicted, and a crypt lair where the lich king and vampire queen await the rising of the next age of darkness.

The Ritual Hall

Blood-crusted grooves score the black granite altar at the room's center. If the party carries any hook that involves the altar (Cleric, Paladin, Warlock), this is where it pays off. The altar can still be fed to grant a dark boon, at a cost you set (drained max HP, a corrupting mark, a debt owed to the Court).

Hidden Sconce — Ritual Hall

Built into a recessed niche along the curved wall, concealed behind carved stonework until a creature moves past the bend. DC 15 Perception to notice before it fires (lower than a searching check should find it — this is meant to catch the party off guard as they round the corner). Once triggered, it functions identically to the Antechamber's Magical Sconces.

Initiative+3
HP80
AC5
Range25 ft.
Attack+4 to hit
Damage1d4+4 necrotic
Fire RateEvery round
RechargeRound 5 (no actions)

Disable. DC 14 Arcana or Dexterity (Thieves' Tools). A failed attempt causes the crystal to explode for 8 necrotic damage (DC 14 Dex save for half).

Reuse the Magical Sconces stat block from Part One rather than reprinting it — just note the niche and the ambush trigger here.

The Crypt: Boss Encounter

Lich King Malqor Veyth (King-Consort of Ossyrra) and Vampire Queen Seraphyne Thalussyrr (Queen of Ossyrra) wait at the center of the crypt, entombed but not destroyed.

  • Malqor Veyth was once a titled member of Ossyrian elite, an ambitious necromancer, and one of the few among the living upper class who supported Queen Seraphyne's continued rulership after her undeath.
  • Seraphyne Thalussyrr survived a vampire attack on her family's castle; forever changed, her undeath left her the only survivor. She was determined to retain her crown when many sought to invalidate her claim because of her undeath.

A note on stat blocks: don't scale down the actual Lich (CR 21) or Vampire (CR 13) for this fight. Even "weakened," those blocks assume abilities and complexity built for a much higher-level fight, and trimming legendary actions on the fly mid-session is a recipe for a rough table. Instead, both bosses below are reskins of lower-CR blocks that already match the behavior the script calls for — no improvised nerfing required.

Malqor Veyth — "Lich King"

Archmage energy trapped in an undead husk. Use the SRD Mage stat block as-is, reflavored: swap damage types to necrotic and reskin the spell list (chill touch instead of fire bolt, etc.). This reads as lich-adjacent for the table without importing lich-specific mechanics like Turn Resistance, Rejuvenation, or legendary actions.

CR6
AC12 (15 w/ mage armor)
HP40 (9d8)
Speed30 ft.
Attack+5 to hit (spell)
Save DC13

Reflavored Spells. Chill Touch (as fire bolt, necrotic, prevents healing on hit), Ray of Enfeeblement or Blight (as cone spells), Vampiric Touch or a necrotic-flavored fireball-equivalent for the big AoE. Keep the Mage's spell slots/cantrips as printed — only reskin damage type and descriptions.

Role in the fight: ranged control and area denial. Stays at range, uses the room's sconces as free actions (see below), retreats behind Seraphyne if approached in melee.

Seraphyne Thalussyrr — "Vampire Queen"

Use the SRD Vampire Spawn stat block as-is — no reflavoring needed. It already grapples and drains, which is exactly the melee behavior the script calls for.

CR5
AC15
HP82 (11d8+33)
Speed30 ft., climb 30 ft.
Attack+5 to hit (claws/bite)
Damage1d8+3 slashing / 1d6+3 + 2d6 necrotic (bite, grapple only)

Regeneration. Regains 10 HP at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 HP and isn't in sunlight or running water. Role in the fight: closes into melee immediately, grapples and drains the party's weakest-looking member, exactly as scripted.

Running the fight: This is your boss room, so treat it as a two-phase encounter rather than a straight stand-and-swing.

Phase 1: Awakening. The party enters mid-ritual. Malqor and Seraphyne (stat blocks above) begin bound to the altar and can't move for the first 2 rounds. Use this window for a warning volley (necrotic bolts, spectral hands) rather than full attacks.

Phase 2: Free. Once unbound, both act at full strength. Malqor leans on ranged necrotic spells and commands the room's crystal-powered sconces as free actions (reuse the sconce stat block). Seraphyne closes into melee and tries to grapple/drain the party's weakest-looking member.

Lair action (round-end, no roll needed): the crypt's ceiling groans. Trigger one Falling Rubble hazard automatically each round the fight continues past round 3, escalating the pressure to end things.

Quick Escape. From the crypt, the party may find a secret, hidden exit that shortcuts them to the outside. Useful if you want a "grab the win and run" ending rather than a full clear.

Campaign Hook

Don't let the module force a clean kill. If Malqor or Seraphyne is reduced to 0 HP but the party doesn't destroy the phylactery / the coffin isn't staked-and-burned, either monarch can return later as a recurring antagonist. Perfect if you want this one-shot to become session one of something longer.
