Part Three: The Court in Ruin (Dining Hall / Arena)

Other, smaller drains are impassable. The party may exit the sewers into the dining hall, where the next large drain is located, to explore the ruins of the court. There are hints throughout of the royals' perversions.

Once the monarchs' great blood-sport arena, its stone benches now sag under decades of moss and silt. The long dining table nearby is set with corroded silverware, as if a feast were interrupted mid-course and never resumed. Rusted manacles hang from the arena's support columns. The mosaics clue from the Antechamber pays off directly here. This is what they depicted.

Suggested Content

  • A Barbarian's hook payoff: a single surviving ancestral trophy rack, empty but for one weapon. The ancestral blade the Fighter's warlord wants, or a hint toward it.
  • One easy roleplay/investigation beat: a preserved menu or ledger (Investigation DC 12) naming the "guests" who never left, seeding NPC names.
  • A light combat: 2–3 rot-touched hounds or reskinned Broken Guardians, drawn by noise from the dining hall onward.

From here, the party moves toward the throne room and the ritual hall beyond, leading finally to the crypt.

Part Four: Battle in the Crypt

This level contains the ruins of the throne room. It overlooks the dining hall and leads to a ritual hall where waking the preserved king and queen is depicted, and a crypt lair where the lich king and vampire queen await the rising of the next age of darkness.

The Ritual Hall

Blood-crusted grooves score the black granite altar at the room's center. If the party carries any hook that involves the altar (Cleric, Paladin, Warlock), this is where it pays off. The altar can still be fed to grant a dark boon, at a cost you set (drained max HP, a corrupting mark, a debt owed to the Court).

The Crypt: Boss Encounter

Lich King Malqor Veyth (King-Consort of Ossyrra) and Vampire Queen Seraphyne Thalussyrr (Queen of Ossyrra) wait at the center of the crypt, entombed but not destroyed.

  • Malqor Veyth was once a titled member of Ossyrian elite, an ambitious necromancer, and one of the few among the living upper class who supported Queen Seraphyne's continued rulership after her undeath.
  • Seraphyne Thalussyrr survived a vampire attack on her family's castle; forever changed, her undeath left her the only survivor. She was determined to retain her crown when many sought to invalidate her claim because of her undeath.

Running the fight: This is your boss room, so treat it as a two-phase encounter rather than a straight stand-and-swing.

Phase 1: Awakening. The party enters mid-ritual: use a Lich stat block for Malqor (or a scaled-down "Lich, Weakened" — drop legendary actions to two and remove Turn Resistance if your table is lower-level) and a Vampire stat block for Seraphyne. They begin bound to the altar and can't move for the first 2 rounds. Use this window for a warning volley (necrotic bolts, spectral hands) rather than full attacks.

Phase 2: Free. Once unbound, both act at full strength. Malqor leans on ranged necrotic spells and commands the room's magical sconces as free actions (reuse the sconce stat block). Seraphyne closes into melee and tries to grapple/drain the party's weakest-looking member.

Lair action (round-end, no roll needed): the crypt's ceiling groans. Trigger one Falling Rubble hazard automatically each round the fight continues past round 3, escalating the pressure to end things.

Quick Escape. From the crypt, the party may find a secret, hidden exit that shortcuts them to the outside. Useful if you want a "grab the win and run" ending rather than a full clear.

Campaign Hook

Don't let the module force a clean kill. If Malqor or Seraphyne is reduced to 0 HP but the party doesn't destroy the phylactery / the coffin isn't staked-and-burned, either monarch can return later as a recurring antagonist. Perfect if you want this one-shot to become session one of something longer.

GM Tool

If Zyra Kethis is in the party, she was gifted with hearing the final cries of the dying. She heard Seraphyne's family die in the vampire attack that made her — everyone in that castle except Seraphyne herself. Whether and how she reveals it during this fight is entirely up to the table.
