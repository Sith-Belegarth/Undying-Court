The Ossyrran Blade
Longsword Hilt — Rare — Requires Attunement

"The hilt looks like nothing. That's the point. Everything that made this sword a legend fits in your palm, not the grip."

What It Does

While grasping the hilt, you can use a bonus action to cause a blade of solidified energy to spring into existence, or make the blade disappear. While the blade exists, this magic longsword has the finesse property. If you are proficient with shortswords or longswords, you are proficient with this weapon. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

The Crystal

The hilt itself is inert — a plain, unremarkable grip that could pass for mundane. Its power comes entirely from a single crystal socketed into the pommel, and attunement is to the crystal, not the hilt. You can move your crystal freely between any compatible hilt you find. Removing a crystal takes an action and doesn't damage it; attuning to a new one follows the normal attunement rules (a short or long rest spent focused on it), and you can only be attuned to one crystal at a time.
